What the app does
------------------------------------------------------------
Atlanhix is an open-source VPN/proxy client (Flutter + sing-box/Xray
engines). It creates a Android VPN tunnel (android.net.VpnService) to
route the device's traffic through proxy servers the user configures
from their own subscription links. No data collection, no analytics,
no ads. Full source: https://github.com/alimc98/atlanhix
